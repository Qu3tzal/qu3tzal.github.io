A management/exploration game on the theme of Polynesian culture.
The idea is to make a game that helps us understand and feel the legends and stories of navigators that conquered the Pacific.
We want to have a game that work in multiple iterations:
- arriving to a new island and settling
- surviving and developping the group
- exploiting the local resources and start sending scouts
- once decided, take the chance to jump to the next island using some of the scouts information
- navigate to the next island using navigators skills

This is both a sim-city/management game when settling and developping the current island but also a risk taking game where trying to jump to the next island requires good preparation and risk taking.
The end goal is to reach the island of Hawaii, often considered as the legendary paradise island.
